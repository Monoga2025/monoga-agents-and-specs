## Active decision filters
For each project, ask:
- Is there a real user, client, deadline, or revenue path?
- Is there a smallest finishable outcome within the next work cycle?
- What is already working and can be reused?
- What dependency or ambiguity blocks completion?
- Can this be paused without losing valuable context?

## Classification vocabulary
- `active`: currently receiving focused work and has a concrete finish condition.
- `revenue`: directly supports a sale, delivery, or cash collection.
- `personal-unab`: academic or personal work with a defined need.
- `paused`: intentionally deferred with a recorded reason and restart condition.
- `archive`: historical or reference material preserved but not active.
- `review`: ambiguous, duplicate, stale, or requiring a human decision.
