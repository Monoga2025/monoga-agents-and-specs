## Conclusion: there is no real prospect list

No event-associated buyer list can be built from public sources. The rows above are relationship or channel contacts, not buyers.

## Why nothing is higher than low

- Speakers and organizers are mostly sellers of events/education, not typical buyers; they are more useful as relationship or channel contacts than customers.
- No sponsor or exhibitor list for the 2026 event was found.
- The partners on the academy site (Maxwell Leadership, Forbes, Hotmart, INCMty, Universidad Rafael Landivar) are the organizer's general associations, not confirmed event sponsors, and are large organizations unlikely to be early buyers. They are intentionally not listed as prospects.
- The academy site lists named students with revenue claims (testimonials). These are individuals; they were not profiled or added. They are only evidence that the community includes owners of digital businesses.

## Better use of this research

The real prospect pool is the attendees, who cannot be identified in advance and must be met in person with consent. Prepare for the event with `ICP_MAP.md` and `EVENT_PLAYBOOK.md`; do not build a pre-event target list from the testimonials page.
