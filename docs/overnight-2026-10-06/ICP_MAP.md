# ICP map - Luque Business Summit (Nov 2026)

Everything here is HYPOTHESIS built from the thin evidence in `EVENT_RESEARCH.md` (organizer claims: owner-led businesses, digital-launch culture, USD 295 ticket, Strategy/Systems/Tactics agenda). Validate each ICP with real conversations before treating it as true. The "2,000+ owners / 12 countries" figure is an organizer claim, not verified. A USD 295 ticket shows capacity to pay for training, not for COP 10M projects. First-pilot hypothesis is aligned with `SALES_KIT.md`: pilot COP 1.5M-2.5M (14 days), then COP 600k-1.2M/month, low end for the first three pilots. Other figures below are placeholders to test, not quotes. Quoted "owner language" is hypothetical wording, not heard from real owners.

Monoga offers: AI sales agent (WhatsApp/lead qualification), AI ops agent (task and process automation), AI command center (BI dashboard). Existing demos are prototype-stage; do not claim production references.

## ICP 1 - Digital entrepreneur / info-product launcher (probably the largest slice, but NOT the best first bet)

- Typical company: online academy, coach/consultancy, agency, digital-service brand run by owner + 2-10 people. EVIDENCE of the type: organizer's programs target this profile (luqueacademy.com/programas).
- Probable size: HYPOTHESIS USD 5k-100k+ monthly revenue, team under 15.
- Expensive problem: leads from ads/launch land in WhatsApp/Instagram DMs and are answered late or inconsistently; launch week overload; no closing follow-up.
- Observable signals: ads running, link-in-bio with WhatsApp button, "agenda tu llamada", launch calendar, one setter/closer.
- Caution: this is the organizer's own business model; these buyers are likely saturated with AI-setter vendors and sell the same promise, so differentiation is hard. ICP 2 is the better first bet (hypothesis).
- Fit: **AI sales agent** (drafts replies and qualifies leads from owner-approved answers; a person approves what is sent) plus light command center for launch metrics.
- Owner language: "Se me enfrian los leads", "mi equipo no alcanza a contestar en lanzamiento", "no se cuanto me cuesta cada venta", "dejo plata en la mesa por no hacer seguimiento".
- Objections: "Un bot suena a robot" - answer: nothing is sent without your team's approval; it drafts from your approved answers; "Ya uso un CRM" - answer: it works on top of what you use; "Es caro" - answer: start with a 2-week pilot measured by response time and booked calls.
- Starting ticket (hypothesis, per SALES_KIT.md): pilot COP 1.5M-2.5M + COP 600k-1.2M/month.
- Highest-impact demo: the 3-minute local Lead Desk demo in `SALES_KIT.md` (synthetic leads, scoring, consent handling, draft reply for human approval, follow-ups). Not a production WhatsApp integration.

## ICP 2 - Local owner-led SME that grew past the owner's capacity (Santander / Colombia) - hypothesis: the better first bet

- Typical company: commerce, services, light industry, clinics, workshops, distributors (the cohort in `docs/PROSPECT_RESEARCH.md` is of this kind, not event-confirmed). Event attendance by this type is likely but unproven.
- Probable size: HYPOTHESIS 10-60 employees, COP 50M-1,000M monthly sales.
- Expensive problem: processes in heads, WhatsApp, Excel; quotes, orders, collections, follow-ups drop; owner is the bottleneck. Matches the Day 2 "Systems" theme (EVIDENCE of theme, hypothesis of fit).
- Observable signals: owner asks "como delego", staff turnover mentions, many Excel sheets, quotes sent by chat.
- Fit: **AI ops agent** (order/quote tracking, reminders, internal assistant) + automation of repetitive admin.
- Owner language: "Todo pasa por mi", "se nos pierden pedidos", "el cliente pregunta y nadie sabe en que va", "cartera atrasada".
- Objections: "Mis empleados no lo van a usar" - answer: start with one process and one person, minimum steps; "Mis datos" - answer: scope and data access agreed in writing, no real data in demos; "No tengo tiempo" - answer: 30-minute diagnosis first.
- Starting ticket (hypothesis): free or low-cost diagnosis, then test the SALES_KIT pilot range (COP 1.5M-2.5M, 600k-1.2M/month); higher only after a measured pilot.
- Demo: synthetic order/quote tracker where a customer asks status and the agent answers from a task board; owner sees overdue items.

## ICP 3 - Owner who "feels blind" about numbers (multi-branch / growing, hypothesis)

- Typical company: 2-5 branches or several sales channels, fast growth after training.
- Probable size: HYPOTHESIS COP 150M-2,000M monthly sales, 20-100 people.
- Expensive problem: decisions on gut feeling; margins, stock, sales per seller or channel not visible until month-end. The event's Day 1 "next growth opportunity" requires data (theme EVIDENCE, need is hypothesis).
- Observable signals: owner asks for "tablero", mixed tools (POS, Excel, invoicing), monthly reporting delays.
- Fit: **AI command center** (consolidated BI, alerts, weekly digest).
- Owner language: "No se cuanto gane realmente", "cual sucursal me da plata", "mi contador me da los numeros tarde".
- Objections: "Tengo que cambiar de sistema" - answer: it reads existing exports first; "Mis datos son sucios" - answer: part of the first phase; "Ya tengo reportes" - answer: show the decision a report did not support.
- Starting ticket (hypothesis): not quotable yet; needs a diagnosis. Do not anchor above the SALES_KIT range for a first-time sale.
- Demo: synthetic dashboard with a 1-minute "what changed this week and what to do" digest.

## ICP 4 - Professional-services / education company with a delivery team (agency, consultancy, academy backoffice)

- Typical company: sells programs or projects, with onboarding, support and renewals. Organizer itself (55 collaborators, claim) is a model of this type; do not pitch the organizer unless a human relationship exists.
- Probable size: HYPOTHESIS 10-50 people.
- Expensive problem: support and onboarding questions repeated over WhatsApp/email; student or client drop-off; manual reporting.
- Observable signals: community groups, repetitive FAQs, large WhatsApp communities, support inbox.
- Fit: **AI ops agent** for onboarding/support triage, plus sales agent for renewals/upsell.
- Owner language: "Los alumnos preguntan lo mismo", "se nos caen en la mitad del programa", "mi equipo de soporte esta saturado".
- Objections: "Pierdo el toque humano" - answer: the agent answers FAQs and escalates emotional or complex cases; "Calidad de respuestas" - answer: answers only from your approved material, with human review of edge cases.
- Starting ticket (hypothesis): not quotable yet; same rule as ICP 3.
- Demo: FAQ + onboarding assistant over synthetic course material, with escalation to human.
