## 2. Inferences (HYPOTHESIS)

- The audience skews to **owner-led small/medium businesses and digital/info-product entrepreneurs**, because the organizer sells launches, "money" frameworks and scaling coaching. Many attendees are likely to be solo operators or teams under ~20 people. Confidence: medium.
- The USD 295 ticket (about COP 1.1-1.3M at an assumed 3,800-4,300 COP/USD; check the current rate) signals an audience able to spend roughly one month of a small salary on training. It is **not** evidence of capacity for a COP 10M+ project. Team pack of 3 and 10+ corporate packs suggest owners bring staff. Confidence: medium.
- The agenda (Strategy / Systems / Tactics) primes attendees to think about "systems" on Day 2 and "quick improvements" on Day 3: the natural windows for an operations or sales-automation conversation.
- Digital-launch culture means many attendees already run WhatsApp, Instagram, Hotmart-style funnels and ads. Their pain is probably **lead follow-up speed, WhatsApp chaos, and no single view of numbers**, not "no website".
- Attendees come from 12 countries, so Bucaramanga is probably a minority of the audience; local follow-up in person will not scale to all. Confidence: low-medium.
- Expect attendees to be saturated with sales pitches from other vendors and speakers' own programs. A diagnosis-first approach differs from the "program upsell" of the stage.
